IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING LIVE:
1. Replace placeholder phone, email, domain and business information.
2. Replace demo product data/prices with your authorized inventory and licensed images.
3. Connect Razorpay merchant account. Razorpay supports UPI, cards, netbanking and more.
4. Use a backend to create/verify Razorpay orders and validate webhook signatures. Never put the Razorpay secret key in browser JavaScript.
5. Connect a database for customers/orders/inventory.
6. Connect shipping provider (e.g. Shiprocket) and real tracking.
7. Add final GST/invoice, privacy, terms, refund, shipping and consumer-policy text after professional/legal review.
8. Configure domain, HTTPS, email and analytics.
9. Test COD, prepaid, failed payment, refund, cancellation and address validation before launch.

The current checkout intentionally does NOT charge real money.
